Privacy Policy

Jair Video Uploader · Effective August 27, 2026

1.Who is responsible

Jair Filho ("the Operator") is the data controller for the Jair Video Uploader application ("the Application"). Contact: jair.filho@pitang.com. This policy is written to meet Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018).

The Application is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance.

2.What data the Application handles

Only what is needed to place a video draft in your TikTok account:

  • TikTok account identifiers — your open ID, display name, and avatar URL, received from TikTok under the user.info.basic permission. These confirm which account an upload belongs to.
  • Authorization tokens — the access token and refresh token TikTok issues when you authorize the Application. These are credentials, not personal content, but they are treated as sensitive.
  • Video files you submit — held only for as long as the transfer to TikTok takes.
  • Technical logs — timestamps, request outcomes, and error messages, used to diagnose failed uploads.

3.What the Application never collects

  • Your TikTok password. Authorization happens on TikTok's own screens; the Operator never sees your credentials.
  • Your existing videos, drafts, direct messages, comments, or follower lists.
  • Your contacts, location, device identifiers for advertising, or biometric data.
  • Any special-category data as defined by the LGPD.

4.Why this data is processed, and on what basis

Account identifiers, tokens, and video files are processed to perform the service you asked for — the legal basis is execution of a contract with you (LGPD art. 7, II). Technical logs are processed on the basis of the Operator's legitimate interest in keeping the integration working and secure (LGPD art. 7, IX). No data is processed for advertising or profiling, and no automated decisions are made about you.

5.Who the data is shared with

The only third party that receives data is TikTok, because TikTok is the destination of the upload. Its handling of that data is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy.

Data is not sold, rented, licensed, or traded. It is not shared with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics vendors. It may be disclosed only if legally compelled by a valid order from a competent authority.

6.Storage, security, and location

Tokens are stored encrypted at rest and are never written into logs, error messages, or URLs. Access is limited to the Operator. Transfers to TikTok use HTTPS. Data may be stored on infrastructure located outside Brazil, in which case the transfer relies on the safeguards set out in LGPD arts. 33–35.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that presents a relevant risk to you, the Operator will notify you and the ANPD as required by LGPD art. 48.

7.How long data is kept

  • Video files — deleted as soon as the upload to TikTok completes or fails.
  • Tokens — kept while your authorization is active, and deleted when you revoke it. TikTok access tokens expire after 24 hours and refresh tokens after 365 days regardless.
  • Account identifiers — deleted when you revoke authorization or ask for deletion.
  • Technical logs — kept up to 90 days, then deleted.

8.Your rights

Under LGPD art. 18 you may ask the Operator to confirm whether your data is being processed, to give you access to it, to correct it, to anonymize, block, or delete unnecessary or excessively processed data, to port it to another provider, to delete data processed on the basis of consent, to tell you who it has been shared with, and to withdraw consent. Write to jair.filho@pitang.com; requests are answered within 15 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados).

9.Revoking access

The fastest way to stop all processing is to revoke the Application inside TikTok: open your TikTok profile settings and find the section that manages connected apps and permissions, then remove this Application. The Application's tokens stop working immediately, and the Operator deletes the stored tokens and identifiers.

10.Children

The Application is not directed at children or adolescents and is not intended for anyone under 18. If the Operator learns that data belonging to a child has been collected, it will be deleted.

11.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Material changes affecting how your data is used will be communicated before they take effect where the Operator has a way to reach you.

12.Contact

Data protection questions and LGPD requests: jair.filho@pitang.com.
